Stop me from doodling on company time zine

“Stop me from doodling on company time” is a collection of notepad doodles from work at the ice cream shop. While struggling to find design work, at my part-time job, I still had opportunities to do my own projects, and this was one of them. It was made to commemorate the business closing and to say goodbye to coworkers.
