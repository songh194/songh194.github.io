"gender" book

Gender is something loose, silly, and fun. To me, memes are big parts of my online and offline presence, and I wanted to put this heavy subject of gender in a not-so-heavy design.

I've bedazzled the spreads to communicate the positivity and festivity of the content, while also connecting the internet meme-fied aesthetic to something tacky in the physical world.
