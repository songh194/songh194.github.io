TNS Cribs

TNS Cribs is a new video series from the New School Free Press. It draws inspirations from the classic show MTV Cribs, but about apartment hunting and living in NYC as The New School students. I've got to be a part of it as a graphic designer, creating the visual identity.

These are a few examples of on-campus/digital advertisement of the series, but there are more areas to explore and use the visuals. I also got to design the physical prop rent-o-meter for the show.
